**Overall: C−.** Weighted toward what ships: the public API (C), styling and tokens (C), build and packaging (C−) and the shipped skills (C−). The architecture holds, and the mechanical rules are followed everywhere. But three defects consumers can hit are verified in the published v5.3.0 (unstamped client chunks, `theme.css` shrinking container widths, `cn` dropping role classes), and `asChild` crashes on four components at HEAD. Conventions the rulebook leaves unwritten are applied a different way in nearly every component family.
