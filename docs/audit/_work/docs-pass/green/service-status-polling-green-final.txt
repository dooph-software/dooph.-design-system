The guardrail **"The DS owns no data, fetching, timers or control; state arrives as props"** (from `$dooph-ds-writing-components`) prohibits a DS component from owning the fetch call or the 30-second polling timer. **What the consuming app owns:** the polling logic — calling the endpoint every 30 seconds and passing the current status down as a prop.
